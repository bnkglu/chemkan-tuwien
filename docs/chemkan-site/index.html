Results in brief

3.5·10⁻⁴H₂-air held-out MSE (1150 K, φ 1.3), 344 parameters
1.36 %H₂-air median ignition-delay error, 30 igniting conditions
9.2·10⁻⁴CH₄-air median MSE on 406 unseen conditions, 1,892 parameters
1.16·10⁻³CH₄-air held-out MSE (1550 K, φ 1.3)
2.5× / 4.0×Julia ChemKAN vs Cantera, H₂ / CH₄ (median time per trajectory)
Code, data and runs: github.com/bnkglu/chemkan-tuwien
Based on Benjamin C. Koenig, Suyong Kim, Sili Deng, “ChemKANs for combustion chemistry modeling and acceleration,” Phys. Chem. Chem. Phys. 2025; 27 (33): 17313–17330. doi.org/10.1039/d5cp02009c. The methane extension is part of this project, not the original paper.
